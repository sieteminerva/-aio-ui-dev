# Design Philosophy

The work connects visual design, software, physical production, and music. Do not flatten that identity into a generic developer profile.

Design should be expressive but purposeful. Preserve visual character, editorial rhythm, color relationships, and human voice while making the interface usable and responsive.

The guiding principle is:

> A product has its price, but its packaging adds value.

The broader working motto is:

> We learn, we build, we innovate.

Prefer work that is thoughtful, useful, durable, and honest about its process.

## Interface Requirements

- Support both dark and light color modes.
- Keep the visual language simple enough to remain readable while respecting the existing animation system.
- Design mobile-first because the primary audience may use standard mobile devices.
- Choose typography for readability and hierarchy, not novelty alone.
